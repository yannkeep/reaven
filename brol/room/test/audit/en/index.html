Rule of Law Score · EU criteria applied uniformly

On 12 key rule of law indicators, Belgium scores worse than Hungary on 8 of them.

Belgium is not an authoritarian state. It is something more subtle: a formal democracy whose constitutional mechanisms have been hollowed out by a parallel system — the particracy — with no basis in the Constitution it has colonized.

The First President of the Court of Cassation said it. The IFDH documented it. The ECHR condemned it. Three supreme courts issued a joint memorandum.

The only actor that has not reacted is the Belgian citizen — forced to vote, forbidden to decide.
